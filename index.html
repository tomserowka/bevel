Your trackpad has edges. Put them to work.

Bevel turns the edges and corners of your Mac trackpad and Magic Mouse into controls. Slide for the brightness of the screen you're looking at, or for volume. Swipe for the next track. Double-tap a corner for dark mode. The middle of the trackpad works exactly as before.

Four edges, four corners, no new keys to learn

A touch that starts at the very edge becomes a control within a millimetre or two. Everywhere else, the trackpad is just a trackpad.

Top-left corner

Double-tap to switch dark mode.

Top edge

Swipe to send the front window to your next screen.

Top-right corner

Double-tap to mute.

Left edge

Slide for the brightness of the screen under the pointer. Two fingers change its warmth.

Right edge

Slide for volume, Spotify's while it plays. Hold still to pick the sound output.

Bottom-left corner

Double-tap to lock the screen.

Bottom edge

Swipe right for the next track, left for the previous one.

Bottom-right corner

Double-tap to play or pause, or to run any Shortcut.

Those are the defaults. Every zone can be given another action, and any app can have its own setup, like seeking on the bottom edge in your video player.

More than a brightness slider

Bevel handles the parts other edge tools leave out: several screens, the Magic Mouse, and the moments you need to point at something.

Every screen, on its own

  • The screen you're looking at. Brightness follows the pointer, so each display keeps its own level.
  • External monitors too. Most are driven over DDC/CI, the way their own buttons do it. Anything else is dimmed in software.
  • Darker than dark. Keep sliding past the backlight's minimum and Bevel dims the picture itself, for late nights.
  • Warmth per screen, not one setting for all of them.
  • Follows the room light on external screens if you like, using your Mac's light sensor.

Magic Mouse included

  • Front edge slides volume, left side slides brightness.
  • Front corners take any corner action, such as tiling windows to halves.
  • Tap to click, if you want it: one finger clicks, two fingers right-click.

Windows and apps

  • Maximise, restore or tile the front window from a corner.
  • App switcher on an edge. Hold still to see your recent apps, slide to choose, lift to switch.
  • Seek back or forward in the front app's player.

Draw on screen

  • Hold a shortcut and draw over anything, for calls and recordings. Let go and it's gone.
  • Pen, arrow, ellipse, rectangle and numbered markers, in the colour you pick.

Every slide comes with light haptic ticks, a firmer one at the halfway point and at the ends, and an on-screen display on the screen you're changing.

It never talks to the internet.

Bevel has no network code at all. No account, no analytics, no update pings. It stores its settings on your Mac and nothing else.

Install in a minute

Bevel lives in your menu bar. Open it again from Applications whenever you want its settings.

  1. Download the latest Bevel .dmg.
  2. Drag Bevel into Applications.
  3. Open it. Bevel isn't notarised yet, so the first time go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. Allow Accessibility in the welcome window, so the pointer stays put while you slide.

Requires macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Works with the built-in trackpad, the Magic Trackpad and the Magic Mouse.
