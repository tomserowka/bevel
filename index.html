the gestures

Four edges, four corners, no new keys to learn

A touch that starts at the very edge becomes a control within a millimetre or two. Everywhere else, the trackpad is just a trackpad.

Top-left corner

Double-tap to switch dark mode.

Top edge

Swipe to send the front window to your next screen.

Top-right corner

Double-tap to mute.

Left edge

Slide for the brightness of the screen under the pointer. Two fingers change its warmth.

Right edge

Slide for volume, Spotify's while it plays. Hold still to pick the sound output.

Bottom-left corner

Double-tap to lock the screen.

Bottom edge

Flick right for the next track, left for the previous one. Keep your finger down and slide to seek within the song.

Bottom-right corner

Double-tap to play or pause, or to run any Shortcut.

Those are the defaults. Every zone can take another action, and any app can have its own setup, like seeking on the bottom edge in your video player.

every screen

The screen you're looking at, and only that one

Most brightness tools change one display, or all of them. Bevel changes the one under your pointer.

  • External monitors too. Most take brightness over DDC/CI, the way their own buttons do. Anything else is dimmed in software.
  • Darker than dark. Keep sliding past the backlight's minimum and Bevel dims the picture itself, for late nights.
  • Warmth per screen. Slide with two fingers to warm one display, not all of them.
  • Follows the room. External screens can track your Mac's light sensor, from wherever you last set them.

Point at a screen (or tap it), then drag the edge on the right.

draw on screen

Point things out, right on the screen

Hold a shortcut and draw over anything, in a call or a recording. Let go and it's gone.

  • Arrow, pen, ellipse, rectangle, numbers and a highlighter, from the toolbar or the keys 1 to 6, in three line sizes.
  • Neon ink. Press N and every mark glows like a tube light.
  • Spotlight (7) dims everything but a circle round the pointer. W turns the screen into a whiteboard.
  • Laser pointer (8): a glowing dot with a short tail that fades, and leaves no marks.
  • Undo with Z, clear with C and keep going, or switch on fading ink so marks vanish by themselves.
  • Prefer a toggle? Press once to draw, again to clear.

The Magic Mouse gets the trackpad's tricks, and windows and apps are a slide away.

Magic Mouse included

  • Four edges, like the trackpad: front slides volume, left side brightness, the rest whatever you pick.
  • Four corners take any corner action, like tiling windows.
  • Two fingers on brightness set warmth; hold to pick an output or app, if you want it.
  • Tap to click, if you want it: one finger clicks, two right-click, three middle-click.

Windows and apps

  • Maximise, restore or tile the front window from a corner, or resize it with a slide.
  • Switch tabs, zoom or undo one step at a time as you slide an edge.
  • Summon an app from a corner, and hide it again with the next double-tap.
  • A timer on an edge: slide to set the minutes, lift, and the countdown sits in the notch.
  • Keep awake, a privacy curtain or a screenshot to the clipboard, each a double-tap away.
  • bevel:// links, so Shortcuts, Raycast and scripts can drive it too.
  • App switcher on an edge. Hold still to see your recent apps, slide to choose, lift to switch.
  • Seek back or forward in Spotify or Music, even behind other apps, or in the front app's player.
  • Sound output: hold still on the volume edge, slide to your headphones, lift.
  • Microphone mute and keyboard backlight, for calls and late nights.
  • Haptic ticks as you slide, with a firmer one at halfway and at the ends.

Lights up as you slide

The edge of the screen glows and fills to the level, like this page's edges when you tried the demo. The display grows out of the notch on a MacBook, and springs from the top as an island on every other screen.

It never talks to the internet.

Bevel has no network code at all. It stores its settings on your Mac and nothing else.

  • No account, no sign-in
  • No analytics or crash reporting
  • Every release ships with SHA-256 checksums

get it

Install in a minute

Bevel lives in your menu bar. Open it again from Applications whenever you want its settings.

  1. Download the latest Bevel .dmg.
  2. Drag Bevel into Applications.
  3. Open it. Bevel isn't notarised yet, so the first time go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. Allow Accessibility in the welcome window, so the pointer stays put while you slide.

Requires macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Works with the built-in trackpad, the Magic Trackpad and the Magic Mouse.

faq

Questions

macOS warns me before opening it. Is that normal?

Yes. Bevel isn't notarised by Apple yet, so macOS asks once, before the first launch. Click Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security. Each release lists SHA-256 checksums if you want to check your download.

Will it get in the way when I use the trackpad normally?

Only touches that start right at the edge become controls, and Bevel decides within a millimetre or two. The pointer stays put while you slide, and a single tap in a corner still clicks as usual.

Does it work with my external monitor?

Most monitors take brightness over DDC/CI, the same way their own buttons do. Apple displays use the system's own brightness. Anything that supports neither is dimmed in software.

Which permissions does it ask for?

Accessibility, so the pointer stays still while you slide and windows can be moved. Bevel works without it, with fewer features. The dark mode corner and the Spotify and Music controls ask for Automation the first time you use them.

Can I change what each edge and corner does?

Yes. Settings lists every zone with its own action, under a picture of your device that shows your fingers live. Any app can have its own setup too.

How do I remove it?

Quit Bevel from the menu bar and move it from Applications to the Bin. Its only other file is its settings, ~/Library/Preferences/io.github.tomserowka.bevel.plist.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Bevel is free.
